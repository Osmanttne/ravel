Six names. Three scams. Same playbook.

Scam posts in group chats are copy-paste jobs. The sender changes and the wording shifts, but the script, and often the phone number, stays the same. Judged one message at a time, every one of them looks harmless.

Two senders. The same message word for word, and the same number.

Detection stops at one message.

1 One message at a time

Each post is judged alone. Copy-paste scams with new names and wording look harmless every time.

2 Blind to new campaigns

Blocklists only catch links someone already reported. A fresh scam has no history to match.

3 Checking is a hassle

Verifying means searching the number, the link, and the story, or handing an app access to your private messages.

4 Nobody sees the pattern

The same payment link or number can appear across GroupMe, Instagram, and iMessage, and no single person sees all of it.

Share a screenshot. Get clarity.

Ravel is a scam checker built into your iPhone's Share menu. No copy-pasting, no research, and no handing an app access to all of your messages. You choose exactly what to send, and nothing else is read.

Screenshot

Choose one image from Photos, or share it from any app with the iPhone Share menu.

Message

Paste or type the text you were sent. Nothing is read automatically.

Link

Parse a URL without opening it. The destination is never visited.

iMessage

Prefer not to download anything? Text the Ravel agent and get the same verdict back.

Four steps to an answer.

Ravel turns a confusing message into structured evidence, then a strict server-side policy decides what it means. The model never gets the final word.

  1. 1 Share

    Screenshot a message, ad, or conversation, or share a link, straight from any app.

  2. 2 Extract

    Gemini reads the content and pulls out scam signals: urgency, payment requests, requests for passwords or codes, account threats, and links.

  3. 3 Compare

    Coming next: signals are matched against sanitized, opt-in reports from other people to connect a message to known or emerging campaigns.

  4. 4 Verdict

    You get the evidence, the uncertainty, and recommended precautions in plain English, then confirm or reject each flagged signal yourself.

Gemini extracts the claims. Ravel's policy decides the verdict.

Your check stays yours.

Ravel only ever sees the one item you deliberately choose. That is not a setting. It is the architecture.

0 raw messages or images saved
Only what you select

Nothing is read automatically. No inbox access, no background scanning, no contact lists.

Links stay unopened

A link on its own is parsed without being visited, resolved, or sent to AI. Paths and query tokens are dropped.

Nothing kept

Selected content lives in app memory and is cleared when you dismiss the check or the app goes into the background.

Metadata stripped

Screenshots are decoded and re-encoded before analysis, removing embedded metadata from the file.

Keys stay on the server

No AI key ships in the app. Every request passes through Ravel's backend and its strict safety policy.

No silent reports

A private check never creates a community report. Future campaign matching will be strictly opt-in.

In connected mode, the text or screenshot you choose is also sent to Google Gemini for analysis under Google's provider terms. A link checked on its own is never sent to Gemini.

Engineered to be checked.

One backend serves both the iPhone app and the iMessage agent. Gemini returns typed observations, strict parsing validates them, and the verdict comes from Ravel's own policy.
